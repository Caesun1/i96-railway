Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you close?
We can often close in as little as 7 days because we pay cash and don't rely on bank financing. If you need more time, we'll close on whatever date works best for you.
Do I need to make any repairs?
No. We buy houses completely as-is — whether they need a little cosmetic work or a full rehab. You don't need to clean, fix, or stage anything.
Are there any fees or commissions?
None. There are no agent commissions, no listing fees, and we typically cover standard closing costs. The offer we make is what you walk away with.
Is my cash offer an obligation?
Not at all. Requesting an offer is 100% free with zero obligation. Review it, think it over, and only move forward if it's the right fit for you.
What kinds of situations do you help with?
Inherited properties, foreclosure, divorce, relocation, tired landlords, major repairs, vacant homes, and more. If you need to sell quickly and simply, we can likely help.
